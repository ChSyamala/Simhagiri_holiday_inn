Your Stay

Your Comfortable Stay
in Gajuwaka

Simhagiri Holiday Inn is a private one-bedroom holiday home suitable for individuals, couples, families and small groups, with accommodation for up to six guests. Set in Gajuwaka, Visakhapatnam, it offers a quiet, air-conditioned space to relax after a day of travel, with a kitchen on hand for when you'd rather cook than go out.

Every corner is arranged for everyday comfort — clean linens, easy seating, and a layout that works whether you're travelling alone or with the whole family.
